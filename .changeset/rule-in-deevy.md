A Gate is now ruled on in deevy, and nowhere else. A comment on the record that starts `/approve` or `/reject` is now an ordinary comment: it rules nothing, and deevy no longer answers it. The comment deevy writes when a Gate waits now ends with "Rule on it in deevy:" and the Gate's link, where it used to say what to type. The Ruling is still said back on the record. Four-eyes Checkpoints, Approvals and Needs me are unchanged.

A Gate's message in a Project's Slack channel now carries a link to rule on it in deevy, and no Approve or Reject buttons. It is still changed in place when somebody rules. A message posted before the upgrade keeps its buttons until somebody rules on its Gate, and a click on one now rules nothing. Slack now sends deevy nothing, so a Slack Socket holds its bot token and no signing secret. In your Slack app you can turn off **Interactivity** and remove the `/deevy` command; `docs/slack-manifest.yaml` now asks only for `chat:write`, `channels:read` and `groups:read`. A Slack Socket's page no longer shows a delivery address, a secret or deliveries, and `/hooks/<socket>` now answers a request for it with 400. Connecting Slack now takes the manifest and the **Bot User OAuth Token**, and refuses a signing secret.

A Human's accounts on your tools no longer mean anything to deevy. **Settings › Identities** is gone, with linking an account, `/deevy link` and its codes, and Linear's account link. So are a Notion Socket's **Take a verified address as proof** switch, `sockets.update`'s `identityByEmail`, and `identities.*` (`/api/identities`). A Linear application now needs one callback URL, `<BETTER_AUTH_URL>/hooks/<socket>/setup`; you can remove `/api/identities/linear/callback` from it. A Ruling (`gates.get`, `gates.list`) no longer reports `via`, `socket` or `verifiedBy`, and `gate.approval`, `gate.approved` and `gate.rejected` Events no longer carry `via`. deevy no longer appends `gate.ruling_refused`, `identity.linked` or `identity.revoked`. The linked accounts stay in the database, unused, until a later release of this series drops them.

deevy now signs Humans in with GitHub, Google, Microsoft or your own OpenID Connect provider. GitLab, Linear, Slack and Atlassian are no longer sign-in providers: `GITLAB_CLIENT_ID`, `GITLAB_CLIENT_SECRET`, `GITLAB_ISSUER`, `LINEAR_CLIENT_ID`, `LINEAR_CLIENT_SECRET`, `SLACK_CLIENT_ID`, `SLACK_CLIENT_SECRET`, `ATLASSIAN_CLIENT_ID` and `ATLASSIAN_CLIENT_SECRET` are now ignored, and so are those names in `DEEVY_SIGN_IN_ORDER`. A Member who signed in only with GitLab or Slack keeps their membership and signs in next with another of these providers at the same verified address, which links them back. Linear and Atlassian never said an address was verified, so a Member who signed in only with one of them cannot be linked that way; a later release of this series starts from an empty database, and they are invited again then. The sign-in page now shows every provider you configured, with no **More ways to sign in**. The sign-in relay now relays GitHub, Google and Microsoft. GitHub's sign-in no longer asks for `read:org`. An account on another address is no longer linked onto a signed-in Human: two sign-ins are one Human only on one verified address.

Who may join is now a verified address at an allowed email domain, or an invitation. `github_org` and `gitlab_group` allowlist rules are gone: `allowlist.add` refuses them, and a rule of either kind saved before is no longer listed and admits nobody. Members who joined by one stay Members.

If you build your own Socket module or call the core's Socket functions yourself: `@deevy/core/sockets` no longer has the `ruling` kind of `InboundEvent`, `parseRulingCommand`, `IdentityScope`, `AccountLink`, `LinkedAccount`, `ChatInteraction`, `ChatReply` or `ChatActor`. A `SocketModule` no longer has `identityScope`, `vouches` or `accountLink`, an `ExternalActor` no longer has `email`, and a `ChatSocket` is `post`, `update` and an optional `listChannels`, without `verifyInteraction`, `normalizeInteraction`, `answer`, `askForNote` or `respond`. `applyInbound` no longer takes `vouches` or `identityScope`. A module whose tool delivers nothing declares the new `webhookSecretFrom: "none"`, which `sockets.providers` (`GET /api/sockets/providers`) now reports for Slack. `@deevy/sockets` no longer exports `normalizeSlack`, `gitlabIdentityScope` or `GitlabOptions`, and `socketModules` no longer takes `gitlabSignInIssuer`.
