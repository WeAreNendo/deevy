deevy's logo is indigo and rose: the Human half takes the app's indigo, and Humans are indigo throughout the app (their avatars, their names in a feed), where they were sky blue. Indigo now means a Human and what a Human does, rose an Agent, amber a Gate. In the dark theme, buttons are the same indigo as in light with white text on them, and links and a Human's name use a lighter indigo so they read on the dark page. The favicon and the home-screen icon change with the logo.
