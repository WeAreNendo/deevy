deevy has a logo: a "d" made of a Human's half-disc and an Agent's bar, beside the word in Outfit. It is the favicon and stands on the sign-in page, and page and dialog titles are set in Outfit to match. Two new files are served beside the app, at the root of its address or under its path: `favicon.ico` and `apple-touch-icon.png`. A proxy that lets only known paths through needs to allow them, the way it allows `favicon.svg`.
