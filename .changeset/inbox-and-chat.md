deevy no longer sends anything to a Human: no inbox, no email, no Slack direct message, no reminder. What waits on you is **Needs me**, which lists exactly the Gates you may rule on, and the chat channel your Project posts its Gates in. The tracker tells you the rest, as it always has. The **Inbox** is gone from the sidebar, with its count and the `g i` shortcut, and so are **Settings › Channels**, **Settings › Email** and **Settings › Notifications**. **Needs me** used to read only the newest page of open Gates, so a Gate waiting on you could be missing behind Gates waiting on others. `gates.list` with `mine: true` now looks further back for it.

A Project now has a **Chat** section, under **Settings › Projects**. Pick the Slack Socket, then pick a channel from the list Slack gives, or type the channel's id. Saving posts one line in the channel first, to prove deevy can post there. If it can't, for example because the app isn't in that private channel, the binding is refused with Slack's own reason and nothing is saved. Each Gate on that Project then posts one message with **Approve** and **Reject**, and deevy updates that same message as approvals come in and when the Gate is decided. A click rules when the Slack account behind it is linked to a Human under **Settings › Identities**; anybody else is told privately why their click did nothing. A Slack room or incoming webhook you added under **Settings › Channels** no longer gets anything, and neither do routing rules. To keep a team's Gates in a channel, bind that channel on each Project. Removing a Slack Socket unbinds every Project that posted through it, and each Project's history says so. Pausing it keeps the binding, but a Gate asked while it is paused is not posted, and the Chat section says so. A Gate deevy could not post after six tries is recorded in the Socket's history. In the API, `projects.update` takes `chat: { socketId, channel }`, or `chat: null` to stop. A Project now returns `chatSocketId` and `chatChannel`. `sockets.channels` (`GET /api/sockets/{socketId}/channels`, `deevy sockets channels`) lists a chat tool's channels: it returns `channels`, or `channels: null` with a `problem` when the tool would not say.

The Slack app manifest now asks for `channels:read` and `groups:read`, which let deevy list your channels, and no longer asks for `im:write`. To pick channels from a list, add the two scopes under **OAuth & Permissions** in your app's settings on api.slack.com, then reinstall the app to your workspace. Until you do, type the channel's id, from its details in Slack, and the Chat section says why. Add the app to a private channel before you bind it.

An invitation is no longer emailed. The dialog shows the link once, with **Copy the link**, and you send it however you like. `invitations.create` no longer takes `send` and no longer returns `emailStatus`, `emailError` or `emailNotSent`, and a listed invitation no longer says whether its email went. An invitation emailed before the upgrade still works.

The inbox is an Agent's now. `inbox.list` and `inbox.markRead` answer `FORBIDDEN` to a Human. An Agent is still told of a record it was assigned, a comment that mentions it, and a Ruling on its Gate (`run_answered`), as before. `inbox.unreadCount` and `inbox.markAllRead` are gone, and so are every `channels.*`, `routing.*`, `preferences.*` and `email.*` operation. Each of their routes answers 404, and their `deevy` commands are gone. deevy no longer appends `channel.*`, `routing.updated` or `email.*` Events, so a webhook subscribed to them gets none. The ones already in your log stay. **Settings › Event log** files webhook Events under **Webhooks**.

Unset what configured email and reminders, since nothing reads it any more: `DEEVY_EMAIL_SENDER`, `DEEVY_EMAIL_FROM`, `RESEND_API_KEY`, `POSTMARK_SERVER_TOKEN`, `POSTMARK_MESSAGE_STREAM`, `SENDGRID_API_KEY`, `MAILGUN_API_KEY`, `MAILGUN_DOMAIN`, `MAILGUN_REGION`, `AWS_SES_REGION`, `AWS_SES_ACCESS_KEY_ID`, `AWS_SES_SECRET_ACCESS_KEY`, `SMTP_URL`, `DEEVY_GATE_REMINDER_HOURS` and `DEEVY_DEV_STUB_EMAIL`. On Cloudflare Workers you can remove the `send_email` binding named `EMAIL` from your wrangler configuration. A sender saved under **Settings › Email** is no longer used. Its sealed credentials stay in the database, unread, until a later release clears them, and so do your Channels, routing rules and notification preferences. A hosted deployment no longer reads `DEEVY_HOSTED_EMAILS_PER_DAY`, and a Workspace's status no longer reports `emailsToday` or `emailsPerDay`.
