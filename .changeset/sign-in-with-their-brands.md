**The sign-in buttons now look like the providers they sign in with.**

- **GitHub** is GitHub's dark button with its mark, and turns light on a dark page.
- **Google** is Google's own "Sign in with Google" style, in its light and dark themes, with the four-colour G its guidelines require.
- **GitLab** is charcoal with the orange tanuki.

A generic OpenID Connect provider keeps a neutral button with a key, since it could be anybody's. The buttons' labels are unchanged.
