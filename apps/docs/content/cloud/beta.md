deevy Cloud is open by invitation while it is in beta, and we're letting teams in a few at a time. A
Workspace on deevy Cloud runs the same deevy that is released for hosting yourself, unmodified.

## Join the waitlist

Either of these puts you on it:

- On [deevy.dev](https://deevy.dev/), under **Let us run it**, enter your email and choose
  **Join the waitlist**.
- At [app.deevy.dev](https://app.deevy.dev/), choose **Join the waitlist** under the sign-in buttons.
  If you sign in before you're invited, deevy Cloud offers you the waitlist then too.

We keep the address you give and when you gave it, and use it for one thing: to write to you about the
beta, and to invite you when there's room. We don't share it, sell it, or add it to any other list. To
come off it, write to [privacy@deevy.dev](mailto:privacy@deevy.dev).

## Once you're invited

An invitation is for an email address. Sign in at [app.deevy.dev](https://app.deevy.dev/) with a GitHub
or Google account that has that address, and [ask for a Workspace](/get-started/ask-for-a-workspace/).
GitHub or Google has to have confirmed the address; if it hasn't, the sign-in is refused and says so.

Only the person who asks for the Workspace needs an invitation. Your team signs in to the Workspace
itself and joins through its own invitations and rules ([Invite your team](/get-started/invite-your-team/)).

## What the beta means

- **It is free** while it is in beta.
- **One Workspace each.** Each person has one Workspace at a time. One that is being deleted doesn't
  count, but bringing it back needs the room again ([Delete a Workspace](/cloud/delete-a-workspace/)).
- **Daily limits.** Each Workspace can make 50 invitations and send 500 emails in any 24 hours, because
  every Workspace sends through one shared sender ([Daily limits](/cloud/limits/)).
- **We look after it.** Every running Workspace is backed up each night and kept up to date with deevy's
  releases. The version yours runs is at the foot of every Settings page, with a link to its source.
- **It is yours to take home.** Export your Workspace whenever you like and run it on a deevy of your
  own ([Export your Workspace, or take it home](/cloud/export-and-leave/)).
