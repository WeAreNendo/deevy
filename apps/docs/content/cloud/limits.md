During the beta, each Workspace can make **50 invitations** in any 24 hours. Each invitation lets a stranger
in, so the limit leaves room for a team, not for a mailing list. Nothing else is limited by the day.

A day here is the last 24 hours, rolling, not a calendar day: there is no midnight when the count goes
back to nought.

## Invitations

Every invitation counts the moment it is made, whether it is accepted or revoked afterwards. At 50,
**Create invitation** is refused with a message saying when you can invite somebody again, such as:

> This Workspace can make 50 invitations a day, and has. You can invite somebody again in about 3 hours.

To bring in a whole team, add a rule under **Who may join** instead: your email domain or your GitHub
organization lets in everyone who matches it, and rules aren't limited
([Invite your team](/get-started/invite-your-team/)).

## Today's count in the console

In the deevy Cloud console, under **Your Workspaces**, choose **Manage** on your Workspace. Its **Usage**
shows **Invitations today** against the limit, beside its Humans, Agents, Sockets and **Runs this month**.
"Today" there is the same last 24 hours the limit counts.
