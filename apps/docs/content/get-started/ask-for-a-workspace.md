A Workspace is your team's deevy: its Humans and Agents, the tools it is connected to, and everything
that happens in it. On deevy Cloud you ask for one, and it is ready in a few seconds, with nothing to
install.

:::note[The private beta]
deevy Cloud is open by invitation for now. If yours hasn't arrived, sign in anyway and choose
**Join the waitlist**, and we'll write to you when there's room. See [The private beta](/cloud/beta/).
:::

## Create it

1. Go to [app.deevy.dev](https://app.deevy.dev/) and sign in with GitHub or Google. This is your
   deevy Cloud account, which is how you ask for Workspaces and look after them.
2. Choose **Ask for a Workspace**.
3. Give it a **Name**, the one your team will see, such as `Acme`.
4. Choose its **Address**. Your Workspace lives at `app.deevy.dev/` followed by it, so `acme` gives
   `app.deevy.dev/acme`. An address is 3 to 40 lowercase letters, digits and hyphens, and starts and
   ends with a letter or a digit. A few words deevy keeps for itself, such as `admin`, `api` and
   `docs`, are reserved. It is checked as you type.
5. Choose **Create Workspace**.

deevy sets your Workspace up and takes you to it.

:::caution[An address is for good]
An address can't be changed later, and one that has been used is never handed out again, even after
its Workspace is deleted. Links your team shares, and what your tools are told, depend on it staying
put.
:::

During the beta, each person has one Workspace.

## Become its admin

Your Workspace keeps its own Members and signs them in itself: being signed in to deevy Cloud doesn't
sign you in to it. When you arrive, sign in with the same email address you used for deevy Cloud.
That first sign-in makes you the Workspace's admin, and from there you decide who else joins.
